Bargaining Power Plot — Layout Options

x-axis = τL (0 = generator has all power, 1 = load has all power). Which primary story does this figure tell?

Contract Terms
(a) Strike price S [EUR/MWh]
(b) Contract volume M [MW]

A — Contract terms (2 panels)

Shows how S and M shift as bargaining power moves from generator to load. Clean, focused on the contract outcome itself.

Pros

  • Simple story
  • Pairs naturally with risk-aversion heatmaps

Cons

  • Doesn't show welfare distribution

B — Earnings split (2 panels, mirrors existing style)

Mean earnings + CVaR for Generator (top) and Load (bottom) as τL varies. Matches the existing risk-profile notebook cells exactly.

Pros

  • Consistent with existing figures
  • Shows risk exposure shift

Cons

  • Two separate panels to compare
4-Panel: Contract + Surplus
(a) S [EUR/MWh]
(b) M [MW]
(c) δ_G surplus
(d) δ_L surplus
rows = contract terms / surplus split  ·  3 lines per panel @ A_L ∈ {0.25, 0.50, 0.75}

C — 4-panel combined (requires re-running sensitivity)

Contract terms (S, M) plus surplus above disagreement (δG, δL) in one figure. Three colored lines at AL = 0.25 / 0.50 / 0.75. Most complete single figure.

Pros

  • Full picture in one figure
  • Shows A_L sensitivity on τ_L axis

Cons

  • Needs sensitivity re-run
  • Busier layout

Note: Options A & B can be produced from the existing single-A_L data. Option C requires re-running the sensitivity sweep (now configured in YAML). All options use τL on the x-axis.
